Camera, screen sharing, voice and video playback fixes.

## Fixed

- **Cameras work again.** Every camera reached through DirectShow (most virtual cameras,
  and many webcams on Windows 10) failed with "Camera did not provide the selected bounded
  RGB frame". OBS Virtual Camera, DroidCam, OBS-Camera and similar now deliver video, at
  the selected size even when the camera lacks that exact mode. Media Foundation webcams
  accept every buffer layout Windows hands out. Your own camera shows in the call; Spout
  sources stay GPU-only with no local preview.
- **Screen sharing works on Windows 10.** The capture asked Windows to hide its border, which
  Windows 10 cannot do, so the share never started there.
- **Voice no longer cuts out after pauses.** The start of what someone said after a short
  pause could be dropped, and every pause added delay. Speech after pauses now plays in full
  and the delay stays at its 40 ms minimum unless the network needs more.
- **More videos play inline.** Videos larger than 1080p (4K, tall phone recordings) are
  scaled to fit instead of refused, and MP4 videos with Opus audio, as OBS records them,
  play with sound on Windows.

## For troubleshooting

`ascendcord --media-probe` tests every camera and a screen and prints what arrived;
`ascendcord --video-probe FILE` does the same for a video file.
