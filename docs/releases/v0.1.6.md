Discord-style interface improvements, better text rendering, and more reliable calls.

## Added

- **Customize the Appearance settings.** Adjust message font size, member-list width,
  and 12-hour or 24-hour timestamps. Timestamps include the full date on hover.
- **Resize the member list independently.** The channel list and member list keep their
  widths across temporary layout constraints.
- **Open voice settings from the microphone and deafen controls.** Right-click either
  control to open the same menu available from its arrow.

## Improved

- **Keep your call open when another person leaves.** Their departure no longer disconnects
  your device from the call.
- **Render right-to-left text in visual order.** Arabic and other RTL text in bios and
  messages now displays in the expected direction, including mixed English and RTL text.
- **Bring the layout closer to Discord.** Server icons and their rail take less space, with
  more consistent sidebar sizing and message timestamps.
