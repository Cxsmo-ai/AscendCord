# v0.1.14

Cleaner microphone audio, and an audio lab that measures both directions of a real call.

## Fixed

- Microphone audio no longer picks up flutter from capture timing. AscendCord keeps the microphone and the 20 ms network clock in step with a gentle rate correction; it used to react to ordinary callback jitter with up to 0.1% of pitch change, which measured worse than the Opus codec itself. A steady clock now passes audio at exactly its own rate, and real clock drift is still followed.
- AscendCord now hears call members whose client has not reported speaking yet, as long as their encrypted audio can be opened.
- The stereo proof extension no longer sends fields that AscendCord's local verifier rejects.

## Audio lab (opt-in test mode)

- `--test-sweep-channel=<id>` now plays measurement program 3: a 20 Hz–20 kHz sweep, left/right/opposite-phase tones, a level ladder up to +3 dBFS, transient bursts, noise, music and a speech-like voice. `--test-sweep-music=<folder>` adds your own short song clips.
- The AscendCord Stereo Proof browser extension measures what arrives: frequency response, distortion, stereo separation, level linearity, and a null test that subtracts the known signal from what was heard, per song and per frequency band, with pre-echo around transients.
- The browser also plays the program back so AscendCord measures what it receives.
- Runs can go unattended: a Discord tab on the test channel joins by itself, and the finished report is written to `voice-bridge\lab-report.json`. `scripts\audio-lab.ps1` runs a test and prints the result.

Measured on a real Discord call: AscendCord's sending path adds nothing measurable beyond Opus itself at its best settings (510 kb/s stereo), with stereo fully kept.

AscendCord continues to send ordinary stereo Opus packets that standard Discord clients can receive. No listener-side AscendCord feature is required.
