The first public release of AscendCord, a native Discord client written in Rust.

## Highlights

- **Stereo voice without filters.** Opus at up to 510 kb/s constant bitrate in true stereo,
  with noise suppression, echo cancellation and auto gain off unless you enable them.
  Microphones are captured at their native sample rate and resampled cleanly, and packets
  go out on a steady 20 ms clock.
- **App icons.** 19 icon styles for the window, taskbar, tray and the app itself, free to
  switch in Settings → Appearance.
- **Vencord and BetterDiscord themes.** Import a `.css` theme from Settings → Themes and
  AscendCord converts its colours and background into a native theme.
- **Discord-style menus.** Quick reactions, Copy Message Link, server mute, deafen, move and
  disconnect for voice participants, and Copy ID items behind Developer Mode.
- **Lighter.** About 200 MB of RAM in a voice call on Windows, and much less work when
  the window is idle.

## Upgrading from tesktop2

Your settings, extensions and sign-in are copied over on the first launch. The old data
is left in place.
